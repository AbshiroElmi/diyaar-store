Delete your Diyaar Store account

How to ask us to delete your store account, and what happens to your data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@diyaar.so with the subject “Delete my Diyaar Store account”.
  2. Send it from the email address on your account, or include the phone number you sign in with and your store's name, so we can find your account.
  3. We may send a code to that phone number to confirm the request is really from you.
  4. We confirm by email once your account is deleted, within 30 days of your request.
Email a deletion request

Store owners: deleting the owner account closes the store on Diyaar and removes its menu from the customer app. Finish or cancel any open orders first. Earnings Diyaar still owes you are paid out to your payout account before the store is closed.
Staff: deleting a staff account only removes that person. The store and its orders stay.

What we delete

  • Your profile: name, phone number, email and password
  • Staff accounts you added to the store (owner accounts only)
  • Your store profile, logo and photos, menu items and product photos (owner accounts only)
  • Your saved payout accounts (for example EVC Plus or bank details)
  • Your app settings and preferences

What we keep, and for how long

DataWhy we keep itHow long
Completed orders, sales, commission and payout recordsTax, accounting and legal obligationsUp to 5 years, then deleted
Reports of safety incidents or fraudSafety of customers, stores and drivers, and legal claimsUp to 2 years, then deleted
Server backupsDisaster recoveryRemoved from backups within 90 days

Kept records are no longer linked to an active account and are only used for the reasons above.
